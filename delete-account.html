Delete Your TOLET Account

App: TOLET (zw.co.tolet.app) · Developer: TOLET · Last updated: 24 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your TOLET account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This applies to both tenant and landlord profiles, as they share one login.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the TOLET app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the one-time code sent to your phone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email from your registered address, or include your registered phone number:

To: toletzw@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my TOLET account
Include: your full name and the phone number you registered with

Email a deletion request

We may contact you on your registered number to confirm the request is from you. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and email you once it is done.

What we delete

  • Your account and login details (name, phone number, email)
  • Your tenant and landlord profiles
  • Your listings, including photos, locations and descriptions
  • Saved searches and favourites
  • Reports you have submitted that are no longer needed for safety reviews

Any unused wallet balance should be withdrawn before deletion. Contact us at the same email if you need help with this.

Delete some data without closing your account

You can delete individual listings and photos in the app at any time, or email toletzw@gmail.com to ask us to remove specific data.
