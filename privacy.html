TOLET Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

TOLET ("we", "us", "our") operates the TOLET mobile application (package zw.co.tolet.app), which connects tenants looking for rooms and houses to let in Zimbabwe with landlords who list them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your mobile phone number (used to sign in with a one-time code), your name, and optionally an email address.
  • Listing information (landlords): property location, rent, description, photos you upload, and contact details you choose to show on a listing.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's approximate or precise location so we can show rooms to let near you. You can turn this off in your device settings and search by area instead.
  • Wallet and payment records (landlords): top-up amounts, EcoCash transaction references, wallet balances and commission deductions. We do not collect or store your EcoCash PIN or bank card details.
  • Messages and support requests: enquiries you send to landlords or to TOLET support.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs and basic usage data used to keep the app working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and send sign-in codes by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To publish listings and show tenants relevant rooms and houses, including by location.
  • To manage landlord wallets, record top-ups and deduct commission once a property is let.
  • To respond to support requests, prevent fraud and remove misleading or unsafe listings.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing your information

  • Other users: listing details (location, rent, photos and the contact details a landlord chooses to show) are visible to tenants using the app.
  • Service providers that help us run TOLET, under their own privacy terms: Supabase (database and storage), Google Firebase (app services and crash reporting), SMS Pop (SMS sign-in codes), Meta WhatsApp Business (sign-in codes) and EcoCash (wallet top-ups).
  • Legal reasons: where required by Zimbabwean law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users and TOLET.

4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Wallet and transaction records may be kept for up to the period required by Zimbabwean tax and accounting law. Listings are removed when a property is let, withdrawn, or the account is deleted.

5. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from within the app (Profile → Delete account), or by emailing toletzw@gmail.com from the phone number or email linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account, listings and personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as transaction records).

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected users where required by law.

7. Children

TOLET is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights

Subject to the Cyber and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07) of Zimbabwe, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing, by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
